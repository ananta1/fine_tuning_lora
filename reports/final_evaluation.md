## Final Observations
1. **Base Model**: Lacks domain knowledge, gives generic and misleading customer advice.
2. **SFT Model**: High accuracy, but overly technical and leaks internal source code variables and developer tools directly to the customer interface.
3. **DPO Model**: Merges the accuracy of SFT with a professional, customer-friendly support tone. It filters out internal details, formats variables cleanly, and provides clear steps to contact support.
